# Technical References & Foundations: IronGraph-Engine

## 1. Hypertrophy Science & Exercise Physiology
- **Schoenfeld, B. J. (2010)**: *"The Mechanisms of Muscle Hypertrophy and Their Application to Resistance Training"*. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, 24(10), 2857-2872. Establishes mechanical tension as the primary driver of muscle hypertrophy over metabolic stress and muscle damage.
- **Israetel, M., Feather, J., Faleiro, T. W., & Juneau, C. E. (2020)**: *"Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training"*. Renaissance Periodization. Formalizes volume landmarks (MEV, MAV, MRV) and the Stimulus-to-Fatigue Ratio (SFR).
- **Beardsley, C. (2018)**: *"Hypertrophy: Muscle fiber growth caused by mechanical tension"*. Strength and Conditioning Research. Details high-threshold motor unit recruitment and the concept of "effective reps" within 0–3 RIR.
- **Robbins, D. W., Young, W. B., & Behm, D. G. (2010)**: *"The Effect of an Upper-Body Agonist-Antagonist Paired Set Training Protocol on Volume Load and Throw Power"*. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, 24(10), 2873-2882. Demonstrates that Antagonist Paired Sets (APS) cut session time by ~45% with zero loss in volume load or neuromuscular power.
- **Helms, E. R., Cronin, J., Storey, A., & Zourdos, M. C. (2016)**: *"Application of the Repetitions in Reserve-Based Rating of Perceived Exertion Scale for Resistance Training"*. Strength and Conditioning Journal, 38(4), 42-49. Establishes the validity of RIR-based autoregulation for resistance training under acute fatigue.

## 2. Distributed Systems & Graph AI
- **LangChain / LangGraph Documentation (2024)**: State machine cycles, conditional edge branching, and reflection loops.
- **OpenAPI Specification v3.1.0 (2021)**: Full JSON Schema 2020-12 alignment for strict RESTful contract definition.
- **IETF RFC 7807 (2016)**: *"Problem Details for HTTP APIs"*. Standardized format for delivering semantic machine-readable error responses.
